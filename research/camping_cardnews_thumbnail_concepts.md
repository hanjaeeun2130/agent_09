# Thumbnail Image Concepts: "How Not to Be the Campsite Villain"

- Based on: `research/camping_cardnews_manners.md` (Card 1 — Thumbnail)
- Date: 2026-10-25
- Format: Instagram card news, 1:1 square (1080 × 1080 px)
- Thumbnail copy (added later in a design tool): 캠핑장 빌런 되지 않는 법 / 이것만 지켜도 '매너 캠퍼' 인정 ✅ — 꼭 지킬 매너 7가지
- Palette from the plan: forest green (main), warm lantern orange (accent), off-white, red only for "don't" marks

## Concept 1 — Magic-Hour Photo Campsite (photo style)

**One line:** A warm, photo-like campsite at dusk lit by lanterns; the title is added separately in a design tool.

- **Composition**
  - Bottom two-thirds: tent, lantern, camping chairs
  - Top third: dark forest and sky, left empty for the title
  - Bottom right: a small villain element (a distant silhouette blasting a speaker)
- **Color / mood:** Deep forest green + lantern orange; warm and calm
- **Prompt:**
  ```
  Cozy campsite in a pine forest at blue hour, a dome tent glowing with warm orange lantern light, camping chairs and a small wooden table in the foreground, deep forest green tones, soft bokeh, empty dark sky area in the upper third for title text, photorealistic, 1:1 square, no text
  ```
- **Pros:** Has the look of an emotional camping account and makes people want to save it.
- **Cons:** The "villain" wit is weak; the photo alone does not show the manners topic right away.

## Concept 2 — Manner Camper vs. Villain Character Illustration (Top pick)

**One line:** A cute flat illustration in which a manner-camper character gives the villain character a "shh!"

- **Composition**
  - Top: empty space for the title
  - Center left: a smiling manner camper (finger on lips, "shh" pose)
  - Center right: a villain shouting with a big speaker; the red X is added in a design tool
  - Background: a night campsite with tents and a moon
- **Color / mood:** Green, orange, off-white; bright and playful
- **Prompt:**
  ```
  Flat vector illustration of a night campsite, a friendly smiling camper on the left making a "shh" gesture with a finger on lips, a rowdy cartoon villain camper on the right blasting a huge speaker with sound waves, tents and a crescent moon in the background, forest green, warm orange and off-white palette, clean simple shapes, large empty space at the top for title text, 1:1 square, no text
  ```
- **Pros:** The "villain" idea is clear at a glance, and the same characters can carry through cards 2–8 for a consistent series.
- **Cons:** Less of a photo feel; the characters must be kept identical on every card.

## Concept 3 — Do / Don't Split Screen

**One line:** The frame is split in half to compare "the manner camper's site" with "the villain's site."

- **Composition**
  - Left half: a tidy site, soft lantern, quiet mood
  - Right half: scattered trash, car headlights left on, loud music
  - Top center: title; O/X icons are added in a design tool
- **Color / mood:** Left in warm green and orange; right in cold, harsh light with red accents
- **Prompt:**
  ```
  Split-screen comparison illustration of two campsites at night, left side: a tidy campsite with a softly glowing downward lantern, calm and cozy in forest green and warm orange; right side: a messy campsite with scattered trash, harsh car headlights and loud music notes, cooler colors with red accents, clear vertical divide in the middle, space at the top center for title text, semi-realistic illustration, 1:1 square, no text
  ```
- **Pros:** Shows what not to do instantly; the fastest way to get the message across.
- **Cons:** Many elements can make the thumbnail look busy, and the title may get lost on a phone screen.

## Recommendation

**Top pick: Concept 2.** It matches the plan's "villain" wit best, and the same characters can be reused on the other 7 cards.

## Shared Tips

- Every prompt includes `no text`, because image AI often breaks Korean letters.
- Add the title and the red X later in a design tool such as Figma.
- Generating with Higgsfield (`gpt_image_2_5`) costs about 0.25 credits per image and needs the user's approval to spend credits.
