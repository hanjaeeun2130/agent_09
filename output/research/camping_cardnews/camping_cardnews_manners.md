# Card News Plan: "How Not to Be the Campsite Villain" — 7 Camping Manners

- Topic: Camping
- Selected plan: #5 (camping manners)
- Date: 2026-10-25
- Channel / format: Instagram card news, 1:1 square (1080 × 1080 px), 8 cards
- Target: Everyone who uses campsites, especially 20s–30s beginners
- Tone: Light and witty ("villain" meme), but clear and practical

> On-card copy is written in Korean (the post's language), with an English gloss in parentheses.

## Planning Intent

- Night noise is the most common cause of conflict at campsites. Even small sounds travel far in quiet nature, and real cases include late-night music and shouting that led to arguments and staff being called.
- The basic rule for trash is to leave the site "as if you were never there."
- Using the meme word "villain" makes the topic feel relatable instead of preachy, which encourages comments ("I've met this villain!") and shares.

## Card Structure (8 cards)

| # | Role | Content |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Thumbnail | Hook title |
| 2 | Manner 1 | Night noise / quiet hours |
| 3 | Manner 2 | Peg hammering and setup noise timing |
| 4 | Manner 3 | Respect site boundaries |
| 5 | Manner 4 | Lights and car headlights |
| 6 | Manner 5 | Shared facilities (sink, toilet, water fountain) |
| 7 | Manners 6–7 | Trash and fire embers (check before you leave) |
| 8 | Closing | Summary checklist + save/share call to action |

Manners 6 and 7 share one card because both are "before you leave" checks; this keeps the deck at 8 cards.

### Card 1 — Thumbnail

- **Main title:** 캠핑장 빌런 되지 않는 법 (How not to be the campsite villain)
- **Subtitle:** 이것만 지켜도 '매너 캠퍼' 인정 ✅ — 꼭 지킬 매너 7가지 (Follow these and you're a "manner camper" — 7 must-keep manners)
- **Image idea:** A cozy campsite at dusk with warm lantern light; a cartoon "villain" character (blasting a speaker) crossed out with a red X in one corner.
- **Design notes:** Big bold title (2 lines max), high-contrast text over a darkened photo area, "7" emphasized as a large number.

### Card 2 — Manner 1: Keep the night quiet

- **Heading:** ① 밤에는 '매너타임' 지키기 (Keep "manner time" at night)
- **Body copy:**
  - 조용한 숲에서는 작은 소리도 멀리 퍼져요 (In a quiet forest, even small sounds travel far)
  - 음악은 볼륨 낮추고, 큰 소리 대화·고성방가는 금지 (Lower the music; no loud talking or shouting)
  - 매너타임은 보통 밤 10시~아침 7시, 캠핑장마다 확인! (Quiet hours are usually 10 pm–7 am; check each campsite's rules)
- **Image idea:** A Bluetooth speaker with a "volume down" icon; a moon in the background.

### Card 3 — Manner 2: Watch your setup timing

- **Heading:** ② 늦은 밤·이른 아침 팩 박기 금지 (No hammering pegs late at night or early morning)
- **Body copy:**
  - '깡깡' 팩 박는 소리는 생각보다 훨씬 크게 울려요 (The clang of pegs is louder than you think)
  - 늦게 도착하면 최소한으로 설치하고, 나머지는 아침에 (Arrived late? Set up the bare minimum, finish in the morning)
  - 철수할 때도 이른 새벽 소음 주의 (Mind the noise when packing up at dawn, too)
- **Image idea:** A hammer and tent peg with a "zzz" sleeping tent next door.

### Card 4 — Manner 3: Respect site boundaries

- **Heading:** ③ 남의 사이트 가로지르지 않기 (Don't cut through other people's sites)
- **Body copy:**
  - 옆 사이트는 그 가족의 '거실'이에요 (The next site is that family's "living room")
  - 지름길이라도 사이트 안으로 지나가지 않기 (Even as a shortcut, don't walk through)
  - 스트링(텐트 줄)에 걸려 넘어지는 사고도 조심 (Watch out for guy lines; tripping accidents happen)
- **Image idea:** Top-down illustration of campsite plots with a dotted path going around, not through.

### Card 5 — Manner 4: Mind your lights

- **Heading:** ④ 랜턴·전조등이 옆 텐트를 비추지 않게 (Keep lanterns and headlights off neighboring tents)
- **Body copy:**
  - 밤에 켜 둔 차량 전조등은 이웃 텐트에 '조명 폭탄' (Headlights left on at night are a "light bomb" for neighbors)
  - 랜턴은 아래로 향하게, 밝기는 은은하게 (Point lanterns down and keep them soft)
  - 잘 때는 불필요한 조명 끄기 (Turn off unneeded lights at bedtime)
- **Image idea:** A warm lantern pointed downward vs. a harsh beam hitting a tent (do / don't comparison).

### Card 6 — Manner 5: Use shared facilities properly

- **Heading:** ⑤ 공용 시설은 '다음 사람'을 생각하며 (Use shared facilities with the next person in mind)
- **Body copy:**
  - 개수대에 음식물 찌꺼기 남기지 않기 (Don't leave food scraps in the sink)
  - 공원 음수대를 설거지통처럼 쓰지 않기 (Never use a drinking fountain as a dish sink)
  - 화장실·샤워실은 쓰고 나서 깨끗하게 (Leave toilets and showers clean after use)
- **Image idea:** A clean shared sink with a strainer; a small "thank you" sticker style note.

### Card 7 — Manners 6–7: Before you leave

- **Heading:** ⑥⑦ 떠나기 전, 쓰레기와 불씨 체크 (Before leaving: check trash and embers)
- **Body copy:**
  - ⑥ "다녀왔지만 다녀오지 않은 듯" — 쓰레기는 분리수거장에, 노지라면 전부 가져가기 (Leave no trace: use the recycling area, or take everything home if wild camping)
  - 쓰레기 불법 소각은 절대 금지 (Never burn trash)
  - ⑦ 불씨는 물이나 흙으로 완전히 끄고, 남은 숯도 지정 장소에 (Put out embers completely with water or soil; dispose of leftover charcoal at the designated spot)
- **Image idea:** Split card — left: a tidy empty site; right: a fire pit being doused with water.

### Card 8 — Closing

- **Heading:** 매너 캠퍼 체크리스트 ✅ (Manner camper checklist)
- **Checklist:**
  - □ 매너타임 지키기 (Keep quiet hours)
  - □ 늦은 밤 팩 박기 X (No late-night peg hammering)
  - □ 남의 사이트 가로지르기 X (Don't cut through sites)
  - □ 조명은 아래로, 은은하게 (Lights low and soft)
  - □ 공용 시설 깨끗하게 (Keep shared facilities clean)
  - □ 쓰레기는 흔적 없이 (Leave no trash)
  - □ 불씨는 완전히 끄기 (Fully put out embers)
- **CTA:** 캠핑 가기 전 저장📌 함께 가는 친구에게 공유💬 (Save before your trip 📌 Share with your camping buddy 💬)
- **Engagement question:** 여러분이 만난 최악의 캠핑장 빌런은? 댓글로 알려주세요 (Who's the worst campsite villain you've met? Tell us in the comments)

## Design Guide (shared across all cards)

- **Colors:** Forest green (main), warm lantern orange (accent), off-white background; red only for "don't" marks.
- **Fonts:** Bold sans-serif for headings (e.g. Pretendard Bold), regular weight for body.
- **Layout:** Number badge (①–⑦) at top left, heading at top, 3 short lines of body text, illustration at the bottom half.
- **Text amount:** At most 3 body lines per card, each line under about 25 Korean characters, so it reads on a phone.
- **Consistency:** Same character (a friendly "manner camper") appears on each card; the "villain" appears only on the thumbnail and do/don't comparisons.

## Fact Notes

- Quiet hours (매너타임) differ by campsite; "10 pm–7 am" is given as a common example, and the card tells readers to check each site's rules.
- The night-noise, peg-noise, trash and water-fountain points come from Korean news coverage of campsite etiquette; the quiet-hours and clean-site points are also in U.S. state park and campground rules.
