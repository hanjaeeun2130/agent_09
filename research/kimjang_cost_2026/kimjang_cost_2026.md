# 2026 Korean Kimjang Costs: Cheap Cabbage, Pricier Salted Shrimp

- Research date: 2026-10-31
- Scope: Retail prices and year-on-year (YoY) changes for the main kimjang ingredients (aT KAMIS, Oct 2026); government kimjang measures for 2026, with 2025 for reference; the official aT 4-person kimjang cost (2026 status, 2025 results); pre-salted cabbage (절임배추) 20 kg pre-order prices and packaged kimchi prices; causes of the price moves; buying-time and cost-saving tips. Written as source material for a Naver blog post titled "2026 김장 비용 총정리, 배추는 싸고 새우젓은 32% 올랐다".

## Summary

- **Cabbage is cheap, salted shrimp is expensive.** On 2026-10-16 (aT), napa cabbage cost 5,557 won per head (-10.3% YoY) and salted shrimp (새우젓) cost 20,269 won/kg (+32.5% YoY). By 2026-10-30 (KAMIS), cabbage had dropped to 3,969 won (-13.0% YoY), and salted shrimp was 19,025 won/kg (+28.3% YoY). **The "+32%" in the headline is the 10/16 figure.** On the latest data, the increase is about 28%.
- **The claim that radish is "+32%" is out of date.** The +32% retail figure is from 10/6 (YTN). The radish retail price was -7.1% YoY on 10/16 and -1.5% YoY on 10/30. The roughly +32% YoY seen on 10/16 was the **wholesale** price (+31.7%), not retail.
- **No official 2026 aT 4-person cost yet.** A private estimate (Asia Economy, using aT 10/16 retail prices) puts it at **263,929 won (-6.4% YoY)**. The official 2025 aT figure was **201,151 won** (as of 2025-11-17, including discounts, -5.6% YoY). aT gave no traditional-market vs. large-mart split. Other 2025 surveys put traditional markets 10–21% below large marts.
- **Government support.** The Ministry of Oceans and Fisheries (MOF) seafood discount event "Korea Seafood Festa" runs **Oct 28 – Nov 29, 2026**. It offers up to 50% off (20% government-funded, capped at 10,000 won per person) on salted shrimp, anchovy fish sauce, sea salt, oysters and other items, at 56 retailers. The Ministry of Agriculture, Food and Rural Affairs (MAFRA) and MOF plan wider measures **from Nov 9**. Details (rates, limits, channels) had **not yet been announced** as of 10/31.
- **Pre-salted cabbage (20 kg, event-card price):** Emart 29,800 won for the 1st batch and 35,800 won for the 2nd (regular price 49,800 won). Lotte Mart's Haenam box is 29,900 won, about 10,000 won below its 2025 price of 39,990 won.

## 1. Background

- Kimjang is the yearly late-autumn practice of making a winter's supply of kimchi. The standard household benchmark is "4 people, 20 heads of cabbage." aT (Korea Agro-Fisheries & Food Trade Corp.) uses this benchmark for its annual cost survey of 14 ingredients across traditional markets and large retailers [9][10].
- Several groups publish kimjang cost figures with different item lists and dates: aT, the Korea Price Association (한국물가협회), Korea Price Information (한국물가정보) and the Small Enterprise and Market Service (소상공인시장진흥공단). Their totals range from about 200,000 to 480,000 won [7][11][12][13]. Only compare figures from the same survey.
- Demand is shifting toward packaged kimchi. A 2025 KREI survey (based on 2024 data) found that buying packaged kimchi was the most common way households got kimchi [2].

## 2. Current state (as of late Oct 2026)

- **Vegetables are cheaper.** Cabbage, radish, scallions and ginger are below last year's prices. Fall cabbage output is forecast at 1.222 million t (+19.3% YoY), so supply is ample [5][15].
- **Salted seafood is more expensive.** Salted shrimp is up roughly 28–33% YoY. Lower catches are the cause, and MOF does not expect the price to fall before kimjang season ends [1][4].
- **Garlic is mixed.** Different reports and dates disagree (see Section 3). Stock of southern-type garlic is down 7.9% YoY, and MAFRA is considering a garlic discount [15].
- **Pre-orders opened early.** Large marts opened pre-salted cabbage pre-orders in Sept–Oct [2][16][17].
- **Official numbers are still pending.** The official 2026 aT kimjang cost and MAFRA's kimjang supply-stabilization plan have not been released as of 10/31 [7][15].

## 4. Examples / key players

- **aT KAMIS:** official daily retail and wholesale prices; publishes the annual kimjang cost survey (2025: released 11/18) [9][18].
- **MAFRA:** held a kimjang supply review meeting on 10/19/2026; kimjang supply-stabilization plan due in early November [3][15].
- **MOF:** runs Korea Seafood Festa (10/28–11/29) [31][32].
- **KREI (Korea Rural Economic Institute):** forecasts fall cabbage at 1.222 million t (+19.3%) and fall radish at 345,000 t (+3.2%) [15].
- **Large marts:** Emart (29,800 / 35,800 won, 75,000 boxes) and Lotte Mart (Haenam 29,900 won) [16][17][29]. MAFRA said salted-shrimp discounts would come through Emart, Lotte Mart and Kurly in connection with MOF's seafood festival [3].
- **Kimchi makers:** Daesang Jongga (Kimjang Daejeon, up to 15% off) and CJ Bibigo [6][30].

## 5. Trends and outlook

- **Cabbage prices are likely to fall further.** Retail cabbage dropped from 6,256 won (10/6) to 5,557 won (10/16) and 3,969 won (10/30) [2][1][18]. Asia Economy expects total kimjang costs to fall after mid-November. Its reasons are that wholesale cabbage prices reach retail with a lag of over a week, and that cabbage fell about 15% in November 2025 [7]. Producers are worried about oversupply [1].
- **Salted shrimp is likely to stay high.** MOF expects no meaningful decline before kimjang season ends [1].
- **Garlic and onion are likely to stay firm.** Lower storage stocks are the cause, and the government will offset this with stockpile releases and discounts [3][15].
- **Outlook for weather:** MAFRA noted that weather in late October is a risk to prices [1]. October cabbage and radish shipments may dip because of the sharp temperature drop and low rainfall, but supply should be stable from November [3].
- **Structural trend:** more households are buying packaged kimchi or pre-salted cabbage, and retailers are competing harder on kimjang products [2][8].

## 6. Issues and considerations

### Causes of price moves

| Item | Direction | Cause | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| Cabbage | ↓ | Good weather; delayed summer cabbage overlapping with fall cabbage; fall cabbage yield +21.4% YoY | [2][7][15] |
| Radish (retail) | ↓ (after early-Oct spike) | Early Oct: abnormal weather and delayed summer crops. Fall output +3.2% YoY, but 9.6% below normal; heat, drought and heavy rain at sowing delayed growth | [2][15] |
| Salted shrimp | ↑ | Lower shrimp catch; Jan–Aug 2026 output about 9,500 t (-3.5%); 3–6 month aging means the post-July shortfall shows now; low prices last year magnify the % rise | [1][5] |
| Garlic | Mixed / ↑ recently | Smaller crop and storage (southern-type stock about 80,000 t, -7.9%). Premium grades are up; mid/low grades were down in mid-October | [1][15] |
| Red pepper powder | Roughly flat | Dried chili yield +1.3% YoY despite bad August weather | [15][20] |
| Scallion / green onion / ginger | ↓ | Ample supply (no specific cause given in sources) | [1][7] |

### Best time to buy cabbage and how to save on other ingredients

1. **Cabbage: after mid-November.** Prices are falling steeply (-13% YoY on 10/30). They are expected to ease further after mid-November, as southern supply increases and wholesale declines reach retail [7][9]. Buying after the MAFRA–MOF measures start on 11/9 may add discount support [1]. *(The timing advice is my interpretation of the sources, not an official recommendation.)*
2. **Pre-salted cabbage beats raw cabbage on labor.** The event-card price of about 30,000 won per 20 kg box was about 10,000 won lower than in 2025 [16][29]. Check card conditions and whether the 1st batch has sold out.
3. **Buy salted shrimp, fish sauce, salt and oysters during Korea Seafood Festa (10/28–11/29).** It gives 20% government support (up to 10,000 won per person) on top of retailer discounts [31][32]. Prices are not expected to drop later [1].
4. **Shop at traditional markets.** All 2025 surveys found them 10.8–20.7% cheaper than large marts [11][12][28]. Watch for any 2026 Onnuri refund event (2025: up to 30%, capped at 20,000 won). **This is unconfirmed for 2026.**
5. **Garlic:** wait for the MAFRA plan, because a garlic discount is under review [15]. Mid-grade peeled garlic is cheaper than top grade (KAMIS 10/30: 9,813 vs. 12,150 won/kg) [21].
6. **Compare with packaged kimchi.** 40 kg of packaged kimchi costs about 200,000–400,000 won, depending on brand and source [6][7]. Making it yourself costs about 264,000 won in ingredients (estimate) plus labor [7].

### Caveats for reuse

- KAMIS figures change daily. Quote them with their date (e.g. "as of 10/16" or "as of 10/30").
- Treat the aT 2025 cost (201,151 won, after discounts) and the Korea Price Information or Korea Price Association figures (338,000–478,000 won) as separate surveys. Do not mix them.
- Unverified: Lotte Mart 2nd-round prices and dates; Homeplus and Hanaro Mart 2026 prices; the details of the 2026 government measures from 11/9; the official 2026 aT cost; the reason for the 10/16 peeled-garlic price gap (10,150 vs. 10,425 won).
